 Flora The Explorer



About the game

The game is a demo for the OWO haptic feedback suit, the goal is to get to the end of the temple and along the way you get hit by all sorts of traps so you can experience the feeling of the suit.

What i did

I was picked as one of 2 people in each class to go to Malaga! I was in Malaga for 2 weeks, I entered the Malaga game jam and created a game for a gaming company witch was creating a haptic feedback suit.

We only had 2 days to create this whole game so you can imagine the rush we had to go through. I was in charge of the camera cuteness, the audio and the player, I also had the chance again to work with shader graph for the rising sand.

The game is a simple platformer game were you have to jump away from a monster thats chasing you while you have to repair hatches together so you can escape from the monster.

This was a really interesting experience for me, it was the first time I joined a real game jam and the first time I was working with people who were speaking a different language than I. In the end it was more about the experience of making a game and to make a game quickly with completely random people wo can barely understand me and I’m still really thankful that I got the opportunity to join a game jam in a different country and to meet so many new people.
